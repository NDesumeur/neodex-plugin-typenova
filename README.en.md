# TypeNova

The space typing game: words fly across the screen, type them before they reach your rocket.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## How to play

1. On first launch, choose your language (French, English, Spanish or German), then follow the introduction and the tutorial.
2. In the menu, click **PLAY**, choose a mode, then type the words coming from the right. A fully typed word is destroyed.
3. A word that reaches the left edge costs you a life. The game ends when you have no lives left.
4. **Esc** pauses the game. At the end, **Space** starts a new game and **Esc** goes back to the menu.

Typing words in a row without mistakes raises the **combo** and the score multiplier. **Golden words** and **power-ups** give you advantages.

## Modes

- **Normal game**: five difficulty levels (from Easy to X2), with game modifiers you can turn on.
- **Boss fight**: a keyboard duel against NEXUS-7. On your turn you type words to attack, on its turn you type to defend yourself.

## Progression

Every game gives experience. As you level up you unlock rocket skins, cursors, colour themes and typing sounds. The game has 94 achievements, a profile with your records and a history of your games.

In **OPTIONS**: difficulty, typing sound, music, volumes, theme, font (16 to choose from), cursor, language, and the **game guide**.

## In NeoDex

- The game fills the whole tab and is drawn at the size of the window when you open it.
- **QUIT** saves your progress and goes back to the menu: to leave, close the tab.
- When you switch tabs, the game and its music pause.
- Importing your own sounds and fonts, as in the original game, is not available yet.

## Data and sync

Your settings, your progress (level, achievements, records) and your game history are saved by NeoDex, backed up and synced between your PCs if sync is turned on.

## Install

In NeoDex: **NeoStore → Discover → TypeNova → Install**.
